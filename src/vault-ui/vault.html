Welcome to KeyBit

A password manager that keeps everything on this device. Three quick steps:

  1. Create your vault with a master password below.
  2. Import logins from Chrome, Apple Passwords, Bitwarden, 1Password, or LastPass.
  3. Pin KeyBit: click the puzzle icon in the toolbar, then the pin next to KeyBit.

Import

From another password manager

Export your passwords as a CSV file from Chrome, Helium, Edge, Apple Passwords, 1Password, LastPass, Dashlane, or Bitwarden (CSV or unencrypted JSON), then choose it here.

Duplicates already in KeyBit are skipped. Entries without a website are kept as secure notes. Afterwards, delete the exported file: it holds your passwords unencrypted.

Backup

Export

An encrypted copy of your vault. Restoring it needs the master password it was made with.

Automatic backup

Pick a folder, for example in iCloud Drive, Dropbox, or Google Drive. KeyBit saves an encrypted copy there every time your vault changes. On another computer, merge it in below.

Restore

Merge adds the backup's logins and keeps yours; newer copies win, nothing is deleted. Replace swaps your whole vault for the backup.

What keeps your logins

  • Restarting the browser or computer keeps them. They are saved on disk, encrypted.
  • Removing KeyBit from the browser deletes its vault. Keep a backup.
  • A copy of KeyBit installed another way (moved folder, Chrome Web Store) starts empty. Restore your backup there.
